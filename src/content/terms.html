What the service actually does

Extraction reads the text layer and returns tables as JSON. Invoice mode builds an Excel workbook and a checksum. Both are best effort. Scanned or image-only PDFs will not grow a text layer here. Columns are inferred from spacing and separators, so merged cells and awkward layouts can shift. Values stay strings until you parse them. Check the output before you rely on it, pay from it, or import it into a ledger. A PASS checksum is a check of the arithmetic we could see, not a guarantee that the vendor billed you correctly.
